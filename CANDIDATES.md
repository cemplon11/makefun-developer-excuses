# Developer Excuses — candidates

1. It works on my machine, so either ship my machine or stop testing on yours, which is clearly haunted.
2. The bug isn't in my code; it's an undocumented feature that escaped, reproduced, and now has its own roadmap.
3. I didn't break production — I gave the rollback button its first real job interview, and it absolutely nailed it.

## Pick

Candidate 3 — the most original of the three. Candidate 1 leans on the
classic "works on my machine" joke and candidate 2 on the familiar
"it's a feature" joke; candidate 3 reframes a production outage as a
career opportunity for the rollback button, which is a fresher angle,
lands in one beat, and stays well inside the 200-character limit.
